Sources

WASP-76b and HD 189733b (iron rain, glass rain, winds, smell)
  1. 1
    ESO telescope observes exoplanet where it rains ironEuropean Southern Observatory, 11 March 2020. Supports: WASP-76b distance (about 640 light-years), day side above 2,400 °C, night side about 1,500 °C, iron vapor and iron rain, one side always facing the star.
  2. 2
    Blue and bizarreNASA Exoplanet Exploration. Supports: HD 189733b's deep blue color, possible glass particles that rain sideways, winds of about 7,000 km/h, distance of about 63-64 light-years.
  3. 3
    A Nearby Exoplanet Reeks of Rotten Eggs, and That's a Good ThingUniversity of Maryland, July 2024. Supports: hydrogen sulfide detected on HD 189733b by the James Webb Space Telescope.
55 Cancri e (magma ocean, diamond claim, atmosphere)
  1. 1
    NASA's Webb Hints at Possible Atmosphere Surrounding Rocky ExoplanetNASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, 8 May 2024. Supports: about twice Earth's width, very close orbit, one side facing the star, a likely magma ocean, a possible atmosphere rich in carbon gases.
  2. 2
    Planet Made Of Diamond May Not Be So Bling After AllPopular Science, 2013. Supports the limits scene: the 2012 diamond-planet idea, and a later study of the star finding less carbon, which made a diamond layer less likely.
Kepler-16b (two suns)
  1. 1
    From Star Wars to science fact: Tatooine-like planet discoveredSmithsonian Science, September 2011. Supports: a cold, Saturn-sized gas planet circling a pair of stars, temperature about minus 100 to minus 150 °F.
TRAPPIST-1 (seven Earth-sized planets)
  1. 1
    NASA Telescope Reveals Largest Batch of Earth-Size, Habitable-Zone Planets Around Single StarNASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, 22 February 2017. Supports: about 40 light-years away, seven Earth-size planets, three in the zone where liquid water could exist, possibly one side always facing the star, neighbor planets that can look larger than the Moon.
  2. 2
    Webb measures the temperature of a rocky exoplanetEuropean Space Agency, March 2023. Supports: TRAPPIST-1b likely has no real atmosphere, day side about 230 °C, all seven planets would fit inside Mercury's orbit.
Pulsar planets and rogue planets
  1. 1
    Extreme PlanetsNASA Science. Supports: the first planets found beyond the solar system (1992, around pulsar PSR B1257+12), a fast-spinning dead star core firing beams, possible second-generation planets, possible auroras.
  2. 2
    Rogue planets could outnumber the starsThe Ohio State University, August 2020. Supports: rogue planets may be more numerous than stars in our galaxy, and the coming Roman Space Telescope will search for them.
The count and the honest answer
  1. 1
    NASA's Tally of Planets Outside Our Solar System Reaches 6,000NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, 17 September 2025. Supports: more than six thousand confirmed planets, none confirmed to be like Earth, future telescopes built to look for rocky Earth-like worlds.

A note on wording

The trip through a gate is a story device: no one can travel to these planets. Scenes that say "imagine" or "picture" (standing on a moon of Kepler-16b at dusk, eternal noon and midnight on TRAPPIST-1) are dramatized views. Glass rain on HD 189733b, the atmosphere of 55 Cancri e, the auroras around the pulsar planets and the number of rogue planets are leading ideas from models and observations, so the video says "may", "seems" or "scientists think". The "diamond planet" was a headline, not a confirmed fact. Temperatures are rounded and given in degrees Celsius unless stated. The colors of the planets are artistic choices based on the descriptions in these sources.
